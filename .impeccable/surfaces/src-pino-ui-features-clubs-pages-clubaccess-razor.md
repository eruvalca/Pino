[brief](../../docs/features/club-onboarding-access.md) and selected

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** One persistent access home makes identity, club selection, and request
status understandable without losing the person or selected club between steps.

**OWN-WORLD:** Inherit Sideline notebook: navy masthead, white ruled working
surface, cobalt actions, pale-blue selection, readable system sans-serif.

**STORY:** Complete a named profile and cropped photo; create a club or request
coach access; return to a truthful status. Administrators use People to manage
requests and memberships.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A compact masthead above a centered continuous white sheet.
Your club leads, then a completed photo/name strip, a rule, search and readable
club rows with request actions. Creation is a distinct secondary action below.

**FORM:** User-selected access-home composition, grounded candidate five, seed
490995e8. The approved brief preserves hierarchy rather than literal desktop
dimensions or a fictional portrait. A submitted request replaces its task area
with acknowledged status and restrained feedback. Reduced motion keeps feedback.
